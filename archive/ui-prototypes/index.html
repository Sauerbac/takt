HOW TO REVIEW

Look at the work.
Then look at the details.

  1. 01
    Open a direction.

    Use the bottom switcher to move between all twelve full-size previews.

  2. 02
    Inspect its states.

    Scroll below the canvas for empty-document, selection, palette, deletion and inline-editing specimens. Switch Weeks / Dates in the toolbar.

  3. 03
    Pick what feels right.

    Choose one direction, or combine the typography, layout and component details you like from several.

Built around the Takt UX specification.

Week 0 stays fixed. Whole-week bars and week-ending milestones sit inside colored sections. The chart leads; controls stay small. These are visual studies, so chart gestures and editing commands are not implemented.
